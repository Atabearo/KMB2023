
>And now, little children, abide in him, so that when he appears we may have confidence and not shrink from him in shame at his coming.
1 John 2:28

Overwhelming shame kept me from being with my friend, whom I considered to be very good and kind. My imaginary faults towards him caused unwarranted guilt. If I could prevent a person of limited goodness from loving me due to something I didn't do, how much greater is the shame of actually injuring a Person of infinite love? Only by God's grace could I understand that His love, whole and perfect in Himself and moving in hearts who love like Him, is greater than any fault. My confidence is not in myself; I am not worthy because of anything I do. I am loved due to the charity and grace others afford to me. Such is that, at the Lord's coming, I desire to receive Him, with full trust and confidence in His goodness.
