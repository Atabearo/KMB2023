 How long will you wait?
	...when God says, that you *are* the [[salt of the earth]]. You *are* the light of the world. You do not have to wait for permission to do good for the sake of Christ. You do not have to wait for God. He's already chosen you; he already knew your weaknesses and brokenness when he made his choice for you. The ball is in your court, and he has given you the opportunity for freedom, to live a new life.
	Answering the call takes only a "yes" to doing the one thing needful of this moment. It is to say, *"OK, Lord, I'll start here",* in any circumstance you find yourself in. *"I won't let what you did for me go to waste."* And so you *are* distinguishable, salt that brings goodness out of the ordinary and light visible for the world to see. *"I will not let what you started in me, end with me."*
