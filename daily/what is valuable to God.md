What do we have to offer that God does not already have? That is the single, burning desire of the Heart of God, his "thirst": it is for us. It is for your heart.
To give one's heart to God is to entrust him with our very life, and to "love with all our heart, mind, soul, and strength[^2]". To those we love, we give our *time* and *attention*. 

Further, to know and trust God is to believe *in* him. The best evidence for belief is *action*. 

So, the proof of our belief is acting in *faith*:
- to *trust* in his merciful love, and to resolve to always *draw near* to him especially when we turn away.
- to *profess* our faith by our thoughts, words, and actions.
So, the proof of our belief is in the degree we strive to **follow his example.** 
- to merciful love, and showing *mercy* in our thoughts, words, and action.
	- to hunger for justice for life in all its stages, especially the poor and vulnerable.
- to resolve to become Christ-like and *grow in virtue*.
- acting in *love*:
	- receiving... to *thank* and *praise* God in all things.
	- responding... to *offer* what we have.
		- our sufferings for redemptive grace and consoling Christ's Heart
		- our sins for an abundance of mercy and restoration of friendship
		- our joys to share with our beloved friend
