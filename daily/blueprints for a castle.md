### in the next season, [[the first brick]]:
Speaking broadly, I have come to no longer feel the need to apply everywhere in any program across the country, but hone in on one career goal: *to eliminate poverty and hunger in the U.S.*. This is adopted from the mission of the Bill Emerson Fellowship. It is an incredible goal, yet "if someone who makes firm resolutions often fails, what will happen to someone who rarely makes any resolution or only does so casually?"[^1] I have achieved some experience in this field and on programs already, and, if my next employment is as described, I will be being trained in the community health division of my local PHD, working on their programs, including that of nutrition and food insecurity. It will be my **resolute career goal to lead in the anti-hunger space within the next 5 years.** 

Perhaps God will have other plans for me, just as I thought my aspirations for the last 5 years were for integrative medicine. "Man proposes, God disposes"[^2].
