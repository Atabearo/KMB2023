### How to get out of bed:
#### Framework
- 4 reasons to pursue (in)action: desire, values, shoulds, and obligations
- If you want to get out of bed with ease, the "reason to get out of bed" is not a reason why you "should" get out, but that which draws you out. Strong values are those that are connected within yourself ([[ordering values towards integrity]]).
	- Aspect of **agency**: the self-contained choice and action to get up, without waiting. Not in response to emotion or expectation. To move because you chose to do so ([[using my agency]]).
- Hacks, that otherwise don't address the root of motivation:
	- Alarm outside of bed
	- Waking with the sun
	- Sleeping on the floor
	- Knowing "it sucks" for the first 3 minutes, regardless
#### My experience
It matters to me that I am up before 8, and I allow myself to lie as long as I choose to otherwise. Reasons I get up before 8:
- I *desire* to see the sunrise when it's convenient.
- I *desire* to use the bathroom sometimes.
- I *desire* to see my parents before they leave in the morning.
- I *desire* novelty, and there is nothing new happening while I'm inert.
- In spite of my *desire* to stay in bed...
	- I am *obligated* to attend an early meeting.
	- I am *obligated* to clean my house and have other things to do, so I must get up.
	- I *value* the use of my time (*obligation* for stewardship). When I know there is nothing else left for me in bed, I do not waste time.
