### in the end
- There are more disimilitudes than similitudes between God and any analogy with His creation.
In the end, I am most compelled to find my friend at the end of the day and console Him with the little I have. I can never comprehend or know the Creator, but I know enough of what the Father revealed to know He is good. His Son, too, is the same incomprehensible God, but He came down as a man to be like us, and so we can know Him and the love of Our Father. My brothers and sisters before me made it their life to know and love Him. I know little about the large public health field, much less the world and its workings, or even to tell you the significance of my lesser identities in such-and-such time and place. God is constant, the beginning, the now, and forever. He says, "abide in my love!", and so that is the will of God, his new law fulfilling all that has come before. 

Surely these other things are good to think about, but none moreso than these which promote life in perpetuity.
