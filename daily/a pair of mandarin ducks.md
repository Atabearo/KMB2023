> A pair of mandarin ducks are those who share the same heart, desires, and aspirations.

I hold this old friend close to my heart. I once thought that he might succeed in what I wished to do with medical school if I could not make it happen. Since he has come to know God, we now have a shared aspiration greater than any other on earth; now I know for certain we are mandarin ducks, for eternity. Our hearts pursue Him who is Love, and our desire is for heaven. No matter where he goes and even if I never hear from him again, I know we will have our day for reunion. Then, we will catch each other up on all that we have missed.
