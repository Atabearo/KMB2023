
I heard a former Black Panther describe "agency" ([[using my agency]]) as a clear, self-contained decision to act without waiting to be told its allowed, commitment before certainty[^1]. Fr. Mike asked a similar question of becoming a person of character, to live with virture, to choose the Lord: "God is not waiting to act, so what are you waiting for?"

Fr. Mike calls Christians in his "Unshakeable" book to use their *agency* to become virtuous.  

> Doing great things someday is not as important as being a great person today. Doing great things is determined by external factors and opportunities, but being magnanimous can start this very moment. You don't have to wait for your life or circumstances to change, and you don't have to wait for someone to recognize all of your previously overlooked good qualities.[^2]

See these key qualities of agency, in comparison to the section above:
- agency is a clear commitment to executing action
- agency is an internal choice, not determined by external factors/opportunities
- Implication that our actions matter, and our decisions change us. That's agency.
- Does not "wait" to be validated, affirmed, or acknowledged, or "feel ready" first before commitment -- today, this very moment.

Christians have agency to do what is good. The CCC affirms as much that we have the agency make free decisions and are invited by the Church to choose good[^3]. Fr. Mike Schmitz recommends magnanimity practicing virtue deliberately in small ways, such as:

> Honor commitments. Keep your promises. Show up on time; show up when you said you would arrive. Take responsibility without making excuses. Call or text people back when they leave a message. Pay attention to the person in front of you.

And to encounter Jesus, of course, comes with change. He can make me into the saint I am called to be, to ask me to "Live a bigger life. Make a decision. Come awake to the reality that I am really God and that I can make you excellent -- I can make you a great saint".

In some ways, I am becoming the person I want to be, a friend for Christ, every time I make one of these prayer hours, whenever I call upon the Lord, whenever I do the will of the Father. At the same time, I want to also be free to visit him often, decisively. I leave it up to the day-by-day, but to put myself in his path I want to commit to this action fully. 

*How about Eucharistic Adorations, Wednesdays after dinner?*

The Lord will accomplish the work he started in me.
