**God's generosity**.

Because I am Jaime Atadero in the year 2025 in the United States, I may come across pearls of wisdom that were not revealed to saints before me in their time. C.S. Lewis didn't have his writings to ponder. Not all had a Bible to read of their own, or the Catechism of the Catholic Church, or the World Wide Web of information. Not all knew the consoling Divine Mercy that he offers in these times. God reveals to the wise and the simple, generous in his gifts and just in his dealings in every generation. I have a great wealth of treasures at hand; I can not praise and thank God enough, devote myself to His Mysteries enough. My time is best spent not in understanding it all, but *responding to God* in every moment and *using what I have*: the same Spirit and faith that sustains all God's people in love.
