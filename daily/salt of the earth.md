
> "You are the salt of the earth; but if salt has lost its taste, how shall its saltiness be restored? ... Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and give glory to your Father who is in heaven."
> Matthew 5:13, 16

Salt is Jesus' analogy for how a Christian must live in the world. In cooking, salt enhances the good flavors that are already there. Like salt, so are the lay people called to be in the world and participate in the work of bringing out goodness from the ordinary in every time and place ([[presently called]]). Further, salt has a distinct effect on food. It cannot be hidden. A Christian who is indistinguishable from the world is like salt without taste, "no longer good for anything... thrown out and trodden under foot by men" (Mt 5:13).

- I didn't want to live in some places because I desired to serve in an environment that took care of its people and supported common goods and public health.
- How should I approach a place inhabited by broken people or work within a broken healthcare system? As the salt of the earth.
- Just like I could discern the good already present and salted in Waco, so can I enter into any part of the world and seek to bring out good within its people.
