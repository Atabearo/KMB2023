
### Waiting Well series
12/16
	**How does remembering that God has given you a meaningful role in his vineyard change the way you view your work, responsibilities, and daily tasks?**
	Sometimes I find work is beyond my focus. Loose ends, half-finished projects, hastily covered. Before I was aware of God's presence and did his will by my work, it was disheartening to get so little done. Then, I thought that if I was with God, I might become the person I need to be to handle it better. But no: I am still a bumbling mess for a steward. Still, I trust the tasks God entrusts me with. All that He gives me to do is within my power, and even if it is "not much" at all, it is good and its worth comes from trusting obedience. Perhaps these lowly fruits would have been missed by the other workers or these few were most suitable for me to harvest. If it is the will of the Father, than I will do it. I will take refuge in His goodness.
