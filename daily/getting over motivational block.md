## lesson
- We care about the task and must deal with mind
	- Acknowledge that the matter is really, really important to you
	- We need to get started in some way
**2 methods:**
- breaking down (operationalize) big tasks
- Develop degree of detachment
	- appease the perfectionist and get started in some way
		- We can commit to go halfway and get started in acting
		- I know this extension is important, and I will have to draft the email. I'm just going to start on the draft and I don't have to send it

## original instructions, applied
As I sit in front of the big task of "work on CDC application", I instantly lose all motivation and drive to work, even as I am fed, rested, and appropriately stimulated for the work.

1. *Pray for guidance and encouragement.*

Lord, please encourage me in this task. I do want to build the kingdom with you, to save souls from turning to Love rather inwards to their own destruction, and to raise up the ordinary things of creation to your extraordinary light and vision. But while the spirit is willing, the flesh is weak. Strengthen my feet and my hands, my mind and soul, for today's work. I thank you and praise you for prospering the work of my hands.

2. *Acknowledge importance of task*.

But this application is important to me, and it is the work of today, to thank my recommenders and to do myself justice with my best work. 

3. *Break down the task and appease the perfectionist to get started.*

I won't write a spectacular work right off the bat, but I can start working on it today, starting with the elements of the personal statement.
