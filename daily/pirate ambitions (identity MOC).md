
The world of pirates is dangerous and a stage for dueling wills. Pirates are those ambitious enough to take up a flag and live their ideals, even breaking the law and fighting opponents to the death for their way of life. 

What would it look like to take up pirate ambitions today, as myself?

In Oda's world, I might not have the strength of will to join Luffy's crew. My heart might be more aligned anyway to the Hiriluk's sakura-studded flag, in his mission to cure people's hearts. Saved by a miraculous healing, he lived to save others and create miracles. Working in inspired faith, even with lacking medical expertise, his resolve changed the hearts of his friends and opponents. His actions resolutely fell in step with his values. He lived and died worthy of his pirate's flag.

- Where everything, in how I dress, order my room, work on tasks, to eating with others, all is unified under a single ideal
- An overriding principle for life worthy of a pirate flag
	- Would break social norms, by-laws, and legal codes for this life
- Build a crew or community who wants to live by the same ideals
- (To what scale?) Hiriluk wanted to inspire Drum Kingdom, Luffy challenged the entire world to become King of Pirates
	- I, too, want to have worldwide impact--even for one as little as me! 
		- How would I behave if I truly believed that? If I had the faith of a pirate?
			- The *faith* and belief in ideals is something I don't have a grasp on yet

Yet I ought to believe that this ideal is possible, because God himself said it was.
- I want to be a great saint! And bring people all over this world to life
