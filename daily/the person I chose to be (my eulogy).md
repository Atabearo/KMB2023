
My friends, do not mourn for too long over my passing because today is my homecoming, and I have met our Christ face-to-face. I advocate for you from here as I always had on earth. While I pass from you, I pray with all my heart that it is only a little while. For Love endures all things and life to all who abide in it, a lively Love that revives the soul and even the body from the grave, from dust and ashes. This is our calling, to love and abide in Love always; this is our hope in Christ. May we embrace again; I am not far if you know where to seek me.
