> **Agency** is ability to *choose* *and* *act without waiting* to be told its allowed. It is self-contained. Agency is commitment without external certainty. It does not come from an emotional response, but from a deliberate internal response; it is what you do despite what you feel. It is often quiet, saying, "I am responsible for my next move, regardless of circumstances". It is the only thing that produces movement.
> - FBP of Standards, Not Force

#### to live well
And so it is that I choose Christ, I choose to grow into my [[magnanimous self]]. I make this resolution today as my primary foundation: to console Jesus in my thoughts, words, and actions, and to give praise and thanks to the Father for all the gifts of today, especially the crosses. Through Mary's hands and heart, to Jesus' Sacred Heart, and onward to the Father for his greater glory.

Remember also: "man proposes, God disposes". If one who resolves strongly fails, how worse off is the one who resolves weakly or does not resolve at all?

I resolve to put myself in the path of Christ this week in Eucharistic Adoration.
I recognize the opportunity to resolve in my daily lectio divina (recorded in my Daily app) and further in the future when I spend my hour with Jesus.
I recognize my agency to act *in all God calls me to*. Because he calls in every moment, I have agency to respond to his will for me.
I recognize my weakness; though my actions may be little, the little I have I can offer to God with all my strength. In this way, he can and will make of me and my littleness into a great saint. When I offer to receive merciful love and show others mercy, this is the action of saving the world.
I know we belong to another. Therefore, I am responsible to do my part. When I am afraid, I trust in You. When I am too infirmed to move, I trust You to carry me forward.
This is my fiat; may my "yes" to God be one with Blessed Mother's and resound throughout the whole earth.

Especially when I don't feel like doing it-- that is the process of aligning actions to our values rather than our desires, of building integrity ([[ordering values towards integrity]]).

##### in another scenario
What would it look like to claim my agency in my relationship with my old friend?
First, the standard I would like to live to is Christ himself, who humbled himself upon death. I aspire to use my agency to serve God, always. A deliberate response to his call or his will through right authority, rather than a response crying out of my own ego, pride, fear, perceived inadequacy, and other reactive emotions. 
If it is good, timely, wise, and I want to reach out to my old friend, then to use my agency means that *I will act* regardless of external circumstances, resolving to do so even if I *do not* *feel* ready. I am responsible for my next move. God be with us always, even if I stumble. Any act, even failure, done in response to God's will is worthwhile action; to obey the Lord is the greatest service and act of love to our trustworthy and good Dad. 
If I want to give myself in love to my friend, I must have a self to give. To possess myself, to own myself, to claim responsibility takes discipline. Discipline requires using agency. To use *agency in service of the Lord* is to do His will. 
	And that is how a little one is able to love deeply; even though she is weak, she uses what agency she has to do love the Lord; and Love makes her strong.

I would like to use my agency to influence people for good, like Luis does for me. It would require the intention to choose and act. It may require initiative when it counts. 
Further, I would like to broaden this for a career, activating agency on a community level. That's mobilization.
