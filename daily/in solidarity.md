It would seem that the consensus and principle of wisdom for keeping company is to be particular with who you spend your time around, because it makes a difference in shaping your character. But as a Christian, I believe we may be called to something more. Beyond wisdom and this natural law is Christ and His law of love. Christ did not avoid those who were unclean, undesirable, on the margins. He brought light to them like a reverse-contagion; wherever he touched, instead of becoming contaminated, he brought healing. Like the water that flowed from the right side of the Temple, it purifies and brought life to dead waters. God tells us to set prisoners free and attend to those in poverty, to those in material, spiritual, or psychological chains. 

I find myself in a position where I find many of my friends are in such a place. 

I wonder about my Psychology major friend turned Christian, who has come to see the medicines and tools of counselors and psychologists worth little in ultimately helping people. Yes, Christ is the only one who can save us, yet these tools do restore human dignity and bring healing and freedom. God wants us to care for the spiritual needs of our brothers and sisters, but for their afflictions in the world also. Jesus fed the hungry, and healed the sick.
