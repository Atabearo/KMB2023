
The digital infrastructure in its current form is the most powerful manipulation tool in history. Computers have the ability to generate predictions tailored to individual users and are written to shape user behavior towards business interests. The business model of technology is in shaping human behavior en masse, in selling human futures. What gets users engaged is what is most profitable. What is most profitable for business is not always profitable to users or society; algorithms propagate not necessarily what is true, but what brings strong emotions. Extreme views and conspiracies have shown to be profitable, destabilizing democracy and causing outrage, deep polarization, and violence on a societal scale. Negative impacts on an individual from a system tailored to engagement is alienation, loneliness, and negative self-image. Technology needs social regulations because it has the ability to bring out the worst in society. The existential threat is not the technology itself, but what technology can bring about without regulation. 

Knowing this, what is my public stance on social media use and technology? How will I use social media, and suggest for others to balance, for individuals, cons with the benefits of personal social media?
