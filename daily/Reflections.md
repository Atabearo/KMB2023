	 - 6 aspects of empathy:
		 - *Emotion Contagion*: ability to sense that someone is having an emotion that might call for a response.
		 - *Empathic Accuracy*: ability to diagnose emotions, in yourself and in another.
		 - *Emotion Regulation*: ability to work with your emotions without being overtaken, stressed out, or frozen by them.
			 - Contra: empath NS is overwhelmed by emotion contagion -> painfully uncomfortable and dysregulated -> withdraw/fix the problem/lash out (not empathy) 
		 - *Perspective Taking*: ability to ask, "What's it like to be you?" and to see through someone else's eyes.
		 - *Concern for Others*: pre-req to be perceived as empathetic.
		 - *Perceptive Engagement*: PhD level of empathy. The ability to respond to someone else's intense emotion in a way they perceive as care.

#### July
**Be gentle with yourself.**
	It doesn’t need to be soft with yourself, and it doesn’t mean being disciplined. **Being gentle with yourself consists of realize your faults, failings, and weaknesses, and dealing with them appropriately**. God doesn’t want us to beat ourselves up. He wants to press on and try again. In the words of St. Francis de Sales, “Have patience with all things, but chiefly have patience with yourself. Do not lose courage in considering your imperfections but instantly set about the redeeming them – **every day begins anew.**”
	**One of the most important reasons to be gentle with ourselves is because if we can’t forgive ourselves, we will struggle to forgive others.** And if we cannot forgive others or selves, you will resist even God’s forgiveness. We are gentle with ourselves, if we are patient with ourselves, we develop awareness, awareness breeds compassion. And every person who ever crosses your path needs a little compassion.￼
