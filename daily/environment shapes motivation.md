## Concept
Just like salt brings out other flavors in food ([[salt of the earth]]), environmental factors can facilitate and pave the way for other actions. These factors are *permissive influences*. There are 4 types:

- *Social*: what direction do your friends support you to grow? How do they effect your emotions, what thoughts or feelings do they provoke? 
	- Social influences that cultivate the right kind of ambition. Discerning how your people your stimulate you to act.
- *Temporal*: notice patterns of when you are motivated to work, your natural biological clock when you are efficient.
- *Environmental*: facilitating an environment that makes you feel like working (i.e. designated environment for work vs not-work)
- *Cognitive*: Engaging in activities that stimulate your cognition in the right way.
	- Knowing when you can do types of work (creative work, physical activity, connecting, gaming); noticing activity patterns that allow for more work with less cognitive load
