- "the Lord was with him and let none of his words fall to the ground", 
- Jn 10:27: "My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and the follow me", 
- God seeks us out, "that is why I came out"
Proclamation
- Psalmist proclamation of deliverance/faithfulness/salvation/mercy == Jesus' healing and preaching
Knowing and revelation
- the demons knowing Jesus (his true identity) == "I know them (the sheep)" == Jesus' knowing our true identity
	- He reveals who we are, and our mission to reveal who He is
	- "behold, I have not restrained my lips" (Ps 40:9)
#### Meditatio
- (on primal religions): oral tradition is powerful because speech is alive, and requires the speaker's presence. God speaks to us today, just as he did to Samuel and as Jesus says, "I know you". 
- On true identity == God, through his saving grace in Baptism, claims us as a son or daughter of God, our true identity. Just as Jesus knows and reveals our partaking in divine nature, he calls us, like the Psalmist, to rejoice and proclaim this saving help that we have received.
