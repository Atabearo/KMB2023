
> **We weep because we know it could have been otherwise.** ... We weep, knowing how fragile we are. If God is not with us, that fragility means death... that we are destroyed. But when God is with us ... as he is with you right now, there's room in our life for tears, for joy, for joyful tears.
> *Fr. Mike, BIY Day 24: Tears of Joy*

That is today's reflection, recalling the joyful tears at the reunion of Joseph's brothers, of Joseph and Benjamin, with Joseph and his father, Israel. The joyful tears of my forefathers, of all of my brothers and sisters grafted into the lineage of God's people. In our day, the question Fr. Mike poses for us to answer, and to pray with each other about is as follows:

### What joys and sorrows breaks your heart today? 
**...knowing it could have been otherwise?**

Joys:
- Near misses, the joy of the *friendship* between me and Luis. We've talked about this. As impressive as this apparent joy is for friendships with Luis or with Mahita, it is an ever present, hidden joy knowing that *it is so* for my friendships with Via, and MiVi, and Trisha, and Susan (10 years!), and Connor, and Lily, and Tiff... so much joy.
- of God's *grace in friendship* with Ethan, in particular. What could have been fragility, death, and bittersweet memory is a flowerbud destined for eternal bloom.
- of *life*, thinking of my *family*. I could weep for every one of them. Instead, I get to contact them, instantaneously!
- of *eating around a table* with my parents, tasty and nutritious meals made by Dad.
- of *reconciliation*, that grace and favor can be had with God. The grace to be his little daughter.

Sorrows:
- the deaths of Minnesotans, protecting their communities from domestic armed forces. 
- the visions of what could be by means of public health infrastructure run parallel to the sorrows that are. My heart breaks for those left in the cold.
- the hunger and violence, and the personal loss of loved ones, I reflect on in reading the Hunger Games is reflected in the real sufferings in the world. 
- the effect of regional war (Iran, Palestine, Venezuela) and political war (such as in Uganda) on the people, their families, their communities.


*Say more?*
These are the impressions of this moment. If all could be written on the gifts that formed and shape my heart today, I would have novels. Far better is it to eat a daily's bread of gratitude, and respond in the present with thankfulness to God and in respect to those whose lives we are called to cultivate.
