### What it means to be a temple of the Holy Spirit 
- Being a doctor doesn't treat root causes
	- Sirach 38 on doctors, scholars, builders
	- "They keep stable the fabric of the world, and their prayer is in the practice of their trade"
- all human work is valuable
	- sacred and saves the world when brought to God
	- the ordinary is made extraordinary
		- that's faith
- In whatever you do, consecrate it to God
- bringing the presence of God to all
	- practiced in remaining in friendship (grace) with God and practicing charity to others 

### Embodying Christ through mercy
- Klaus: "a true selfless act of goodwill always sparks another"
- Merciful love for grace
	- grace opens hearts for God's presence
		- preparing others to meet God
- Importance of mercy
	- Jesus call to be merciful and love like God
	- How? instrument of mercy
		- saying "yes" to God; letting God love you with His merciful love, and that love and mercy overflow from you to others
			- mercy in deed, word, and prayer
			- incognito Christ
- small spark
	- “I have come to set the earth on fire, and how I wish it were already blazing!" (Lk 12:49)

### A breath prayer towards "praying always"; a merciful prayer for all times 
- the words of Mary: *ecce, fiat, magnificat*
	- humility, your will be done, and magnifying the Lord in praise and thanksgiving
	- essential attitudes 
- conscious of your breathing; use when running, when I see people, before I speak
	- Daniel: "the God in whose hand is your breath"
- see others with the eyes of Christ

### Practicing your faith when you don't feel God's presence
- Fulton Sheen -- "go volunteer"
	- In the experience of prayer you can be deceived, but doing good works never
- perception that if you do not "feel" God or are questioning beliefs that you are a hypocrite
	- feel God and convinced in beliefs first, then practice
		- can't think your way to God
		- you have to take a leap, and then in practice, you find the evidence of Him who was here all along
			- Practice first (what you believe apart from what you feel) with the hope that you will come to feel 
				- Practice marriage: you practice it everyday, you believe in it, and often times you feel it!
- Practicing what you believe even when you are shaken == faith/trust
	- "hope against hope"
		- Blessed is she...
