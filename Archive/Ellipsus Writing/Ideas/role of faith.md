God as the swim teacher?
- Gaining trust
	- "be happy, be scared. It doesn't matter. I'm not here to change your feelings. But you have to stay until the class is over"
		- Have to be in the water, have to take a leap
- To excite the mind to learn something new, it has to be led by the learner, not the teacher
	- *act of will*--acceptance and willingness to take a leap and trust the teacher
		- no one can swim for you. It's you and the water. You have to do it (make the leap and decide for) yourself.
- the same now and forever, the beginning and the end with a singular desire for your heart. Unchanging.
	- "You can trust me"
	- What has to change is that we understand the pattern. 
		- And when we face obstacles, we have gotten to a place where we *decide* that "OK, it's you. Fine, I can do that"
		- When I accept what I can't change, it's better. Realizing, maybe it's OK to not be in control.
- And then you can get the evidence that you can do it comes after. Same with leaps of faith, that the evidence was all around the whole time.
