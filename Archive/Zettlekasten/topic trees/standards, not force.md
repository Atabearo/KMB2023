
Based on the Black Panther Party's organized approach to change institutions not with force, but with intentional, focused *pressure* through *nonviolent* means. 
- Every movement had an strategy: a particular target, an articulate demand, with a specific objective that, by participating in the activity, the BPs expected the target to change according to demand. This creates *pressure* that forces institutions to change.
	- Example activities: sit-ins, boycotts, marches. These were organized, planned actions with objectives.
		- Example targets: police depts, city halls, justice departments
- Element of *nonviolence*
	- Violence creates chaos, and chaos becomes noise that can be leveraged for the use of force and exceptional power.
	- Nonviolence means calm, measured action to build legitimacy, rather than leverage force by violence.
		- Power in legitimacy, not leverage. In changing institutions by standards, not force. 

The government target
- The government is a servant to the people and operates in our communities to basic, civic, legal, and human rights standards. 
	- When government policies do not accord to these standards, we the public have the right to demand accountability and transparency of government institutions. 
		- This is done in a lawful, credible, and effective 
- How institutions respond to change
	- Institutions act when risks to reputational harm is greater than the comfort of silence.
	- This is how change happens quietly and permanently.
	- Example: corporations
		- Corporations respond to risks (investor, regulatory, reputational), not outrage.
		- When approaching corporations with lawful, disciplined, public scrutiny, we build pressure. We increase risk, without confrontation.
	- Example: Energy companies
		- Calm, documenting questions, asking about compliance, community impact, and alignment to stated values
			- These do not disappear; documentation goes to compliance officers, risk committees, and stakeholder boards
		- Consistent and credible scrutiny demands transparency and pressure without escalation
