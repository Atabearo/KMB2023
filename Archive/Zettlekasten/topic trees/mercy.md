
##### Merciful action
> What comfort that anointing must have brought to the very head that within a few days would be pierced by thorns! ... *No sign of affection,* even the smallest, *will ever be forgotten, especially if it is shown to those who are suffering, lonely, or in need*, as was the Lord at that time.
> - Pope Leo XIV in *Dilexi Te*, on the woman who anointed Jesus with perfumed oil
