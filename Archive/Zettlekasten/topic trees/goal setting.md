SMART thinking
- "I am going to study chemistry at 5PM"
	- **Implementation** thought. Not goals, but IMPLEMENTATION orientation -- what, where, how?
Origin of a goal
- I get up in the morning because... "successful people get up in the morning", "everyone else is doing that", "my parents want me to", "because I heard, I watched", I sensed something external to me
- How likely you are to meet a goal depends on where your [[motivation MOC]] is located: internal or external?
