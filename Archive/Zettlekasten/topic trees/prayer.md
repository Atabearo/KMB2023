Prayer is the foundation for a relationship with God, an infallible means of grace. 

##### Pray at all times
Jesus exhorts his disciples to "pray always", to be attentive to God in the same way God is loving us into being at all times. 
Praying at all times (to the best of our ability) begins with dedicated time to prayer ([[monk in the mountains time]]).

##### Need for prayer
"This world of ours is very sick, and only contact with heaven ([[worship]]) will be able to cure it"[^1]
