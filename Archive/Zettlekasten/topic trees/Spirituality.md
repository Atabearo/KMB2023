## Spirituality

Reality
- His singular desire: that all may be with him and brought into His family
	- “God doesn’t ask primarily for perfection, but to live life with him.” TFP
	- 4 kinds of “all” to pray for: people of influence, people close to you, people difficult to pray for, people in your path
- All Love proceeds from God, God’s own spirit
	- Manifests in his creation, in whom His will is done whether we recognize it or not
	    - Ultimate and primary creds go to:
			- All praise, glory, and honor be
			- When we praise the children of God, we direct it to God
	- When we get praised, we ought to direct it to God
-  (JPII) Divine Mercy is the limit imposed by God on evil, the love of God in face of evil 
	- Redemption, merciful love, is stronger than all evil in the world
- [[God is with us]]

	- I am (not) good at ____, and that’s all that means. [link to identity formation?]

	- benefit of the doubt attitude
	- I don’t know anyone else’s heart. But based on what I know about me, I deserve the lowest spot

- Can easily laugh at self [[how to fail]]

For this very reason make every effort to supplement your [[faith]] with [[virtue]], and virtue with [[knowledge]], and knowledge with [[self-control]], and self-control with [[steadfastness]], and steadfastness with [[godliness]], and godliness with [[brotherly affection]], and brotherly affection with [[love]].
