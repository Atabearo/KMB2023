### Origins of motivation from a psychospiritual perspective

| means of satisfaction | Worldly/extrinsic                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Internal/Intrinsic, "on the inside"                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **GAINS**             | **desires** (*want*)<br>	- indriya (sensory impression)<br>	- don't drive motivation, transient<br>	- satisfaction is temporary ("I wanted a promotion when I saw someone else getting one")<br>	- *gratify desires, get a worldly benefit* | **values** (*care about*)<br>	- matter to you within 	<br>	- ACT: therapy to align actions to their values/live in service to values<br>	- *gratify values, internal benefit--take pride in my choices*                                                                                                    |
| **COSTS**             | **shoulds**<br>	- conditioning, internalizing expectations (imposed)<br>	- Low motivation -- that's why it's we "should do" these things, instead of we "do" them<br>	- *bear costs, get a worldly benefit*                                 | **duties**<br>	- "dharma"<br>	- obligations that I myself adopt	<br>	- important to identify because it *allows to do hard things*<br>	- personal cost (but willing to take for the sake of obligation i.e. as a doctor or parent)<br>	- *bear dharma, internal benefit--take pride in dutiful action*<br> |
