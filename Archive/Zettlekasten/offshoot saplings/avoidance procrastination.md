This type of procrastination is driven by avoidance of negative emotions. The state of this brain is one that is *so motivated* to make the problem go away that we choose to avoid it altogether. The negative emotion block acts as a wedge to dealing with the problem constructively.

When we try to overcome this type of procrastination, we often seek to change, but never seek to understand. We don't ask *why* we are procrastinating.

*How to overcome this procrastination?*
If the procrastination is an emotional problem, we need to tunnel down into that emotion, to digest it and become aware of it. Ask, **what are we seeking to avoid?** What consequence are we trying to avoid? What emotions are we feeling or avoiding? 

- Being aware of the problem makes behaving accordingly easier.
