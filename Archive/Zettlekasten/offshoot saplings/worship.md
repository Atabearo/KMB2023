##### Worship
>The worship of the one God sets man free from turning in on himself, from the slavery of sin and the idolatry of the world.
>CCC 2097

Worship, like love, requires sacrifice.
We are made for worship. As Christians, we become temples of the Holy Spirit. Temples are dedicated places of worship.
If the universe is a temple, then we are called to cultivate and keep in whatever we do. In this way, we offer our life as a sacrifice, and thereby glorify the Father and save the world.
The Eucharistic Mass is the ultimate and essential worship, in which we unite our offering with Jesus' offering on the Cross. 
The 3 hours of Jesus' Passion were essential, but all of his work in his 33 years of life mattered. In the same way, our life--our recreation, our work, our livelihood--is an extension of the worship of the Mass. 
By taking worship back into our work, we, like Jesus, redeem the world by coming into contact with it.
*Go in peace, glorifying the Lord by your life*.
