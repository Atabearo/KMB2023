### Responding to a world around us (problem study)
Problem: We are in an undesirable state.
HUSTLE RESPONSE: to hurry towards that place, to work harder to obtain necessary thing in order to be happy. 
	Principle: We need *something* or to *be somewhere* in order to be well.  We do not have what we need and we are not where we are supposed to be.
	Response to reality: recognition of lack turns to concerted efforts to close the gap. Complaining relates poor state. 
PRAISE RESPONSE: in waiting, to know we are cared for in any state, even this one.
	Principle: We have *everything* we need and we are in the *right place* because God is for our good and calls us to our present.
	Response to reality: recognition of providence turns to concerted efforts of faith, hope, and love. Joy relates proper state. 

---
Notes
- Praise is **training** to become *who we need to be* and enables us to endure sufferings, to be patient in trying times. Praise trains us to **wait well**.
- [[God is with us]], so we can praise God now. To praise God [[in all things]] is a declaration that *I have everything that I need* and *I am where I am supposed to be* ([[call to praise]]).

Relation to worry.
- To wait well is to [[trust]] God's goodness and his timing.
- Worry chokes joy, and it doesn't work WHILE feeling like work
