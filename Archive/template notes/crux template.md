### 1. What gifts from God am I thankful for?

## Examen
"Lord, I take this moment to draw close to you. Open my mind and heart to your presence and love. As I pray this Examen, I ask you to reveal your will to me and grant me strength."
#### Ask
- petition for grace:
	- *God, what would you like me to learn in this Examen?*
	- *Are there any specific graces I feel I need, such as the grace of clarity and understanding, fortitude to encounter my weaknesses, or humility to accept God's mercy?*
### Blessings
- *What drew my closer to God?*
- *When did I feel joy and peace today?*
- *How did I see God working in my life today? Note and give thanks.*
>
### Kill
- *When did I struggle to stay close to God?*
> 
### Embrace
- *Receive God's forgiveness and mercy. We can trust His mercy is greater than our wounds, rooted in his unshakeable love.*
### Resolve
- *What is one specific thing I can do tomorrow to better respond to God's grace?*
>
- *Close with the Sign of the Cross andanything else you'd like to share with God.*
