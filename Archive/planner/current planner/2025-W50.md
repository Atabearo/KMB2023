### Journal
- I'd like to do the examen more often -- useful for capturing those specific and tailored lessons from my failures and successes in receiving God well

BAKER after many days
B: always a blessing to talk to Ethan, who seems to take you also as serious as life. I loved getting to meet my extended family and meet Chester and Eric, play with Achi and Shobe, get to know Ate Jas and Tita Remy a little better. Tito Eric and Tita Cora have been very generous in taking care of our family and my mom and dad seem to be very enjoying their time back home. I have been met with much kindness abroad, and I have gotten much time to be with you, too. In the Advent season, I am grateful for learning how to wait well and have you also as my teacher. I have not followed along with the Christmas readings before and find again that Isaiah brings much consolation, especially in tandem with words of hope, waiting, and the triumph of God's reign in the Psalms and Matthew. I am grateful for the good I get to enjoy.
AK: Lord, I desire to be more generous with those suffering, especially those suffering material poverty. I too, make negative judgments in my heart and have remained hardhearted to those asking for mercy while I spent pesos freely. I did not prioritize you as I should at the end of the day, and spent a few evenings choosing to scroll over being with you. I disengaged from the moment and did not end well. I pray that I may entreat you more often for grace, for even my hourly bread, so that I may be with you always. I often felt like a child on this trip, but have I been keeping with my spiritual childhood? 
ER: Resolve again to be totally yours, Lord, and to have you, with Our Blessed Lady, as my guide in all things. I trust in You, Lord; help me to use the faith I have and to put my heart, mind, soul, and strength into consoling You by the way of Merciful Love.
